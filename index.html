About

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at KAIST, working on efficient systems and architectures for generative AI inference.

Research Focus

01
Multimodal LLM Serving

Developing GPU-efficient serving systems for mixed text, image, video, and audio requests that maximize throughput while meeting latency targets.

02
Hierarchical KV Cache Management

Mitigating GPU stalls caused by KV-cache offloading by overlapping communication with computation across the memory hierarchy.

03
Processing-in-Memory for LLM Serving

Addressing system challenges in PIM-based LLM serving, including sparse-attention control divergence and interference between NPUs and PIM.
